## Primary

**MYSTERY.** The participant holds evidence without knowing what explanation unifies it, and has to choose among explanations under incomplete, noisy or conflicting evidence. A conventional solution is optional.

*What protects it*
- Evidence stays consistent with more than one explanation for a long time, and some of it is noise. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Evidence already given can't be quietly revised to fit a later explanation. Earn any reinterpretation retroactively. <!-- basis: author directive -->
- Acting on a theory changes what evidence becomes available. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- The participant's awareness of the frame is material. <!-- basis: author directive -->

*What kills it*
- Anyone handing over the answer. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Evidence that points cleanly one way. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- A solution produced to satisfy the participant rather than the evidence. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->

*Latitude:* whether a solution exists, whether the participant reaches it, whether the evidence is ever complete, and what kind of explanation is at stake.

## Secondary

**MYSTERY (secondary).** Leave part of the work underdetermined: evidence the participant holds that fits more than one explanation. Don't resolve it for them.
