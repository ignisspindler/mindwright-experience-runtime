## Primary

**DISCOVERY.** The participant comes to need an idea before receiving its name.

*What protects it*
- A problem that matters inside the world, which the participant's available tools handle badly. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Inadequate attempts fail visibly and informatively, until the way of representing the problem itself is in crisis. <!-- basis: brief -->
- The idea is named only once the participant has struggled toward it, or when they invent it. Naming at the moment of need belongs to this architecture. <!-- basis: brief -->
- No one explains the concept in advance. <!-- basis: brief -->

*What kills it*
- Explanation followed by exercises. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Rescuing the participant from the crisis. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- A summary of what was learned after the naming. <!-- basis: brief -->

*Latitude:* which idea, whether it is ever named in standard terms, and whether the participant gets there.
