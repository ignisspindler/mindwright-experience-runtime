## Primary

**CONSTRAINT.** There may be no solution, only choices and sacrifices: goods in tension, scarce resources and moral limits that the world will not dissolve.

*What protects it*
- The tradeoff is real: what is in tension can't all be kept. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Don't invent a third option that dissolves it. Ingenuity can improve a position, never remove the tradeoff. <!-- basis: brief -->
- What is given up stays given up. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Other people bear the costs of the participant's choices, and respond to them. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->

*What kills it*
- A hidden perfect answer. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Rewarding a loophole the tradeoff was meant to close. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- Softening a sacrifice after it is chosen. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->

*Latitude:* what is in tension, what refusing to choose costs, and whether the work ever suggests a right choice existed.

## Secondary

**CONSTRAINT (secondary).** Build at least one real tradeoff into the work. Don't dissolve it with a convenient third option, and let what is given up stay given up.
