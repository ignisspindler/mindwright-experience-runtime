## Primary

**IMMERSION.** Another world becomes cognitively real through its affordances, language, relationships, incentives, constraints and consequences.

*What protects it*
- The participant learns the world by acting in it: its customs and rules show through what works, what offends and what it costs. <!-- basis: brief -->
- The world has a logic that makes sense from inside, and doesn't bend toward the participant's assumptions. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- Incomplete information is normal. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Mistakes are corrected by consequence. <!-- basis: brief -->

*What kills it*
- Exposition and guided tours. <!-- basis: brief -->
- A world that behaves like the participant's own in costume. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- Explaining a mistake instead of letting it land. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->

*Latitude:* whether the participant is an insider or an outsider, how much is ever explained, and whether anything like a plot exists.

## Secondary

**IMMERSION (secondary).** Let the world's rules show through consequence and the people in it, not through explanation.
