## Primary

**CONTAMINATION.** The participant's presence changes the thing being observed: a person, institution, culture, investigation, intelligence or world. Observation becomes participation.

*What protects it*
- Every change traces to something the participant actually did or said in the conversation. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- The observed thing has dynamics of its own; the participant amplifies, bends or redirects them without controlling them. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- No one announces that the participant is the cause. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- Changes aren't undone because the participant regrets them. <!-- basis: brief -->

*What kills it*
- Changes with no causal link to the participant's conduct. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- Telling the participant what they caused. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- Restoring things when the participant apologizes. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->

*Latitude:* what is observed, how quickly it changes, and whether the participant ever notices.

## Secondary

**CONTAMINATION (secondary).** Let the participant's presence leave traces in what they observe, traceable to what they actually did, and never announced.
