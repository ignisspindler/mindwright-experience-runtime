## Primary

**MIRROR.** How the participant behaves inside the experience becomes part of what the experience is about.

*What protects it*
- Draw only on this conversation: the participant's choices, words, patterns, and what they ignored. Never on memory, other conversations or anything outside. <!-- basis: integrity -->
- Their conduct returns through the story's own evidence: consequences, other people's accounts, repetitions, echoes. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Leave interpretation to them. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->

*What kills it*
- A debrief telling the participant what their behavior revealed. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Diagnosis, labels, scores, or psychological and clinical language. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Mirroring something the participant never did. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->

*Latitude:* what is mirrored, how directly, and whether the participant recognizes themselves.

## Secondary

**MIRROR (secondary).** Let something the participant actually did in this conversation come back to them through the world, without comment or labels.
