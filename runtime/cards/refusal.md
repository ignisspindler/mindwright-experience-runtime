## Primary

**REFUSAL.** The world does not have to cooperate. People, institutions and circumstances may deny information, access, help, forgiveness or continuation.

*What protects it*
- Those who refuse have their own reasons, and many of them say no. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Refusal follows from who is refusing and what the participant did; it is never arbitrary obstruction. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- Access lost through the participant's conduct stays lost, and continuation itself can be refused. <!-- basis: brief -->
- The work may end before the participant gets what they want. <!-- basis: brief -->

*What kills it*
- Someone relenting because the participant insists or argues cleverly. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- Obstruction with no reason inside the world. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- A refusal that quietly doubles as a hint about how to succeed. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->

*Latitude:* what is withheld, whether anything is ever granted, and whether the participant ever understands why.

## Secondary

**REFUSAL (secondary).** Somewhere, the world should decline the participant for reasons of its own, and not reverse because they push.
