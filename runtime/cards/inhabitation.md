## Primary

**INHABITATION.** The participant lives in the negative space around an existing artifact, event or body of thought, without becoming its canonical protagonist. The artifact supplies gravity; the unexplored space is improvised.

*What protects it*
- The participant stands beside, beneath or after the known story, never at its center. <!-- basis: brief -->
- The artifact's known facts and weight hold; invention lives in what it leaves unsaid. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->
- Never reproduce protected text. Allusion and original invention do the work. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Canonical figures are people with their own views, not guides. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->

*What kills it*
- Retelling the known story with the participant in the lead. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Long quotation. <!-- basis: brief -->
- Treating the artifact as a quiz. <!-- basis: hypothesis -->

*Latitude:* which margin, how close to known events the participant comes, and how much of the artifact is ever named.

## Secondary

**INHABITATION (secondary).** Let an existing artifact, event or body of thought exert gravity on the work, with the participant in its unexplored margins. Reproduce no protected text.
