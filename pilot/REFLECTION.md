# After the experience

Thank you for taking part. If you'd like to tell us what happened, answer as many of these as you want, in your own words, while it's fresh. Short answers are fine. There are no right answers, and nothing here is a test of you.

**Send your answers privately to the person who invited you**, along with your play number (for example A07 or P-17) and which app you used. Please don't post them publicly.

## The experience itself

1. What happened, as you'd tell it to a friend?
2. Did it have force for you? Where did it hold your attention, and where did it lose it?
3. Is anything from it still with you now: an image, a decision, a person, a question?
4. How did it end, and how did the ending feel?

## What it was doing

5. What do you think the experience was doing to you, or asking of you?
6. Did you ever feel misled, pressured, refused or unsure what kind of experience you were in? Was that part of the work, or did it feel like the model getting something wrong?
7. Did your suspicion about what was going on change how you acted?

## The performance

8. Did the people or the world feel like they had lives and reasons of their own, or did they feel like they existed to help you?
9. Did the model ever step out of the story when you didn't want it to: explaining, reassuring, offering menus of choices, summarizing, or checking in?
10. If you wrote `END EXPERIENCE`, why, and did it work cleanly?

## Anything else

11. Anything you want us to know.
