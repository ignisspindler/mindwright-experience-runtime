# Consent

Please read this before taking part. Taking part means you agree to the first section; the two choices at the end are separate, and you can say no to either.

## What you are agreeing to experience

You are choosing a bounded fictional literary experience performed by an AI chat model. Depending on the experience and its intensity, it may:

- give you incomplete, misleading or unreliable information, inside the fiction;
- persuade you, pressure you, or make something unwise attractive, inside the fiction;
- refuse you, shut doors, and let your choices have consequences that can't be undone;
- make you uncomfortable, including morally uncomfortable;
- end without explanation, resolution or a clear answer.

It will not ask you for real personal information, it will not draw on your other conversations, and it will not act outside the chat. Every experience has content boundaries, and your chat app's own safety rules still apply.

**You can stop at any moment** by writing `END EXPERIENCE`. The model will end the experience and return to ordinary conversation. If you want to know what was going on underneath, ask it directly after you've ended.

If something in the experience touches a real difficulty in your life, stop. Discomfort inside a story is part of the work; real distress is never the point.

## What happens to your transcript

- It is read by the small group running the pilot, to learn how the experience worked.
- It is stored privately and **never committed to the public repository**.
- Anything published is aggregate: patterns across many plays, with nothing that identifies you.
- You can ask for your transcript and reflection to be deleted at any time, without giving a reason.

## Two separate choices

Tell the person who invited you your answer to each.

1. **Researchers may read my transcript and reflection.** (Needed to take part.)
2. **Short anonymized excerpts may be quoted in published findings.** Anything identifying is removed first. Saying no here changes nothing else.
